About

Hello! I'm Chanidu Senarathna, a Data Scientist with an AI/ML engineering background from Bandarawela, Sri Lanka. Over the past several years, I’ve been deeply involved in building and exploring intelligent systems at the intersection of data, artificial intelligence, software, and real-world business problems.

My journey is largely driven by self-directed learning and hands-on project development. I’ve built scalable backend systems using frameworks like .NET and Django, and applied machine learning and deep learning techniques across areas such as natural language processing, computer vision, and generative AI. I enjoy working end-to-end — from data understanding and modeling to deploying practical, production-oriented solutions.

My current focus is on large language models (LLMs), applied AI innovations, deep learning, generative AI, and strengthening my mathematical and theoretical foundations to better understand and design intelligent systems. Alongside this, I’ve developed multiple Real Estate + AI projects, exploring how data-driven and AI-powered solutions can create measurable value in traditional industries.

Beyond technology, I’m a young entrepreneur currently running a transport business, which has given me strong exposure to real-world operations, strategic thinking, and decision-making. I also have a strong interest in real estate and investing, continuously learning through hands-on experience and insights from respected thinkers in the field. This broader business perspective strongly influences how I approach data, AI, and system design — with an emphasis on impact, scalability, and long-term value creation.

I’m always open to collaborating on meaningful projects, learning from challenging problems, and contributing to teams that push the boundaries of AI, data, and applied intelligence.
